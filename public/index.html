How the numbers work

Income raises your cash, so what you still need goes down.

Expenses lower your cash, so what you still need goes up. Buying groceries does not increase what you owe anyone, it widens the gap.

Payments lower your cash and the debt together, so what you still need does not move. You turned money into progress.
